Hello there!

I’m a software engineer with more than five years of experience designing distributed systems, microservices, and cloud-native applications across fintech, e-commerce, and enterprise AI. I build high-throughput backend services with Java, Python, Spring Boot, REST, and gRPC, using event-driven architecture to support workloads reaching 10K+ requests per second and millions of daily transactions.

My work spans architecture through production. I design Kafka and Spark streaming pipelines, petabyte-scale processing with Databricks and Delta Lake, and data layers powered by PostgreSQL, Redis, and intelligent caching. In applied AI, I build RAG pipelines, LLM services, semantic search, embeddings, vector retrieval, MLflow workflows, and model-serving integrations that deliver grounded answers with sub-200ms latency.

I operate these systems across AWS, Azure, Docker, and Kubernetes, supported by automated CI/CD and observability through ELK, Prometheus, Grafana, metrics, logs, and alerts. I care about performance, but just as much about resilience, security, and clarity—the best platforms are scalable, fault-tolerant, explainable, and easy for engineering teams to evolve safely.
